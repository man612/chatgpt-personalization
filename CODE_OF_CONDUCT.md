# Code of Conduct

ChatGPT Personalization is an open-source technical project. Contributions, questions, reviews, and disagreements should stay respectful, specific, and focused on improving the work.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- discuss ideas, evidence, code, and outcomes rather than attacking people;
- give feedback that is specific enough to act on;
- assume good faith while still questioning claims and technical decisions;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's personal information;
- accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and project direction without harassment.

## Unacceptable behavior

The project does not tolerate:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory remarks;
- personal attacks or sustained hostile behavior;
- sexualized or otherwise inappropriate content directed at participants;
- doxxing or sharing private information without permission;
- spam, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith engagement;
- pressuring contributors to disclose private reasoning, credentials, client data, or personal information.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces where someone is participating as a contributor or maintainer.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior repeatedly violates these expectations.

For an ordinary disagreement about project decisions, use the relevant issue or pull request. For a sensitive conduct concern, do not post private details publicly; contact the maintainer through the contact methods available on the maintainer's GitHub profile and ask for a private channel.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and prioritize the safety and usefulness of the project community.
