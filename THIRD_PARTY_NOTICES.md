## Humanizer

This repository also selectively adapts a small set of editorial-audit ideas from **Humanizer**, an open-source writing skill by Siqi Chen. The project does not vendor or reproduce Humanizer's full pattern catalog. The adapted ideas are limited to supplemental checks such as drafting residue, staged profundity/candor, formulaic punchlines, present-state documentation, and a bounded second-pass audit.
